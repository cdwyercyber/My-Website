What Is a Computer Worm?

A computer worm is a type of malware that can copy itself and spread between computers without needing someone to open an infected file. This makes worms especially dangerous because they can spread across entire networks on their own, sometimes infecting multiple devices before anything notices!

How Do Worms Spread?

Worms often take advantage of security weaknesses in computers and networks to move from one device to another. Once they get started, they can keep creating copies of themselves and spread without help from anyone!

01. Infect

A worm gets onto a vulnerable device.

02. Copy

It creates copies of itself.

03. Spread

It moves to other connected devices!

What Can Worms Do?

Once a worm infects a device, it can cause quite a bit of damage! Some worms slow down computers by using up system resources, while others can steal information, install additional malware, or spread across an entire network. Even if a worm doesn't directly damage files, it can still create serious problems for the devices it reaches!

Worms vs. Viruses

Worms and viruses are pretty similar, but there is one major difference! A virus needs to attach itself to another file or program to spread, while a worm can copy itself and spread between devices all on its own.
