About Me

Hello! My name is Cole, and I am a student at Olympic College. My goal is to eventually obtain my bachelor's degree in cybersecurity, and work my way up to becoming an offensive cybersecurity professional. Outside of school, I enjoy mountain hiking, swimming, working out, playing video games, and spending time with family!

My Interest in Cybersecurity

What really interests me about cybersecurity is understanding how systems work, finding their weaknesses, and figuring out how attackers eventually take advantage of them. I am especially interested in ethical hacking and penetration testing, and eventually, I would like to work in offensive cybersecurity, either helping organizations identify and fix vulnerabilities before they can be exploited, or by taking down cybercriminal organizations.

What I Am Learning

At Olympic College, I am currently studying networking, security concepts, web development, and ethical hacking. I especially enjoy the hands-on side of cybersecurity, experimenting with hacking tools, exploring vulnerabilities, and learning how different attacks work behind the scenes. This website is a project where I can put some of that knowledge into practice, focusing on different types of malware, and how they work.
