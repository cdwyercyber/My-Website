Welcome to Malware

Malware is one of the most common threats in cybersecurity. It comes in many different forms, each with its own methods, purposes, and ways of affecting computer systems. This website explores different types of malware, how they work, how they are used in cyberattacks, and the threats they pose to individuals and organizations.

Explore Malware

From viruses and worms to ransomware and spyware, there are many ways malicious software can compromise a system. Explore the different sections of this website to learn more about these threats and what makes each one unique.

Understanding the Threats

Understanding how malware works is an important part of cybersecurity. Learning how these attacks happen can help us recognize vulnerabilities, understand attacker techniques, and better protect computer systems.
