What Is Spyware?

Spyware is a type of malware that can spy on someone without them realizing it. It can secretly monitor what someone does on their device and collect private information without their permission. Some types of spyware can even steal passwords, track browsing activity, and collect financial information. The scary part is that it can sometimes sit in the background for a very long time without the user noticing!

What Can Spyware Collect?

Depending on the type, spyware can collect quite a bit of personal information. Some examples include:

  • Passwords and login information.
  • Private messages and browsing activity.
  • Banking and financial information.
  • Keystrokes typed on a keyboard.

How Can You Stay Protected?

One of the easiest ways to avoid spyware is to be careful about what you download and where you get it from. Avoid suspicious links, don't install programs from random websites, and ALWAYS keep your security software updated. You should also pay attention to the permissions apps ask for, because some may want access to things they don't actually need. At the end of the day, it is always better to be extra careful than to accidentally let something spy on you!
